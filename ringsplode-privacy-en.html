🔒 Privacy Policy

RingSplode – Fake Call & Ring Bomber
OrionWavesStudio · Last updated: August 2026

Summary: RingSplode does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. What We Collect

RingSplode does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your phone number or call logs
  • Any audio recordings made via Witness mode (stored locally only)
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Device identifiers

2. Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device and never transmitted:

  • Caller profiles you create (name, emoji, number type, country)
  • Scheduled burst call settings
  • Call history (local log of fake calls triggered)
  • App preferences (haptic, DND, triggers)
  • Audio recordings from Witness mode (Premium)
  • Premium status (verified via Google Play)

3. Permissions Used

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE – Required to run the shake trigger and ringtone service in the background
  • VIBRATE – For haptic feedback during fake calls
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – To display the incoming call notification
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT – To show the call screen over the lock screen
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – To trigger scheduled burst calls at the exact time
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS – To keep the shake trigger active in the background
  • RECORD_AUDIO – Only used by Witness mode (Premium), never transmitted
  • CAMERA / FLASHLIGHT – Only used for the torch effect during ringtone (Premium)
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – To reschedule pending burst calls after reboot

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Free version only): Displays ads. AdMob may collect anonymous advertising identifiers. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing: Handles the one-time Premium purchase. No payment data is processed by RingSplode directly.

Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs. No personal data is included.

5. Children's Privacy

RingSplode is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
